# Welcome to F1 Multiplayer 2016–2026

F1 Multiplayer is built for two friends who want to race a whole career together: the 2016 season through 2026, with the cars, line-ups and ratings of each year. One of you hosts. The server runs on the host's PC, and no EA servers are involved.

## What works today

- **Your own server.** Start it on the **Multiplayer** page. It gets a **six-digit server code**, like `909 914`. On the same PC or network, your friend only types those six digits. Over the internet, they use the longer host code, and the router has to forward one port.
- **Rooms and LAN races** through your server, with an encrypted relay and an addon check, so both of you race with the same mods.
- **MULTIPLAYER CAREER** in F1 25's main menu opens the game's two-player career wizard without the online lock.
- **Classic seasons 2016–2024** and the progressive career from the addons you already have.

## What comes next

1. Invite your friend into the two-player career through your server instead of EA's friends list.
2. Keep the shared career on the host's server, so both of you continue from the same save.
3. The progressive career 2016 → 2026 for two people.

## Testing alone

Only one copy of F1 25 runs per licence. Until a second PC is free, a simulated **test friend** joins your server on the same PC and checks the server, the relay and the bridge to your game:

`f1mp mp test-friend 909914 --probe`

## Built on Swofty

F1 Multiplayer is based on the open-source Swofty Launcher (AGPL-3.0). Addons made for Swofty work here too.
